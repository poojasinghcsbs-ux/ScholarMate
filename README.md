# 🎓 ScholarMate

### AI-Powered Scholarship Management & Renewal Platform

ScholarMate is an intelligent web-based scholarship management platform designed to simplify the scholarship journey for students. It helps students discover suitable scholarships, evaluate eligibility, receive personalized recommendations, track applications, monitor deadlines, and manage renewal requirements through a centralized and user-friendly dashboard.

The platform aims to reduce the manual effort involved in searching and managing scholarships while helping students avoid missed deadlines, incomplete applications, and overlooked renewal opportunities.

---

## 📌 About the Project

Finding and managing scholarships can be challenging for students due to complex eligibility criteria, multiple application deadlines, changing requirements, and the lack of personalized guidance.

Traditional scholarship platforms often provide only basic scholarship information, requiring students to manually search, compare, track applications, and remember renewal deadlines.

**ScholarMate** addresses these challenges by combining scholarship discovery, AI-based matching, application tracking, renewal monitoring, and automated notifications into one integrated platform.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students commonly face difficulties such as:

- Finding scholarships that match their eligibility
- Understanding complex eligibility criteria
- Keeping track of application deadlines
- Receiving personalized scholarship recommendations
- Monitoring application and renewal status
- Remembering renewal requirements
- Missing important updates and reminders

ScholarMate is designed to solve these problems through an intelligent and centralized scholarship management system.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

ScholarMate provides a complete scholarship management experience where students can:

- Create and manage their scholarship profile
- Discover relevant scholarship opportunities
- Receive AI-based scholarship recommendations
- Check eligibility requirements
- Track application progress
- Monitor renewal deadlines and requirements
- Receive reminders for important actions
- Manage their entire scholarship journey from one dashboard
